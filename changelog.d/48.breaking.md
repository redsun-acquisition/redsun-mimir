The example sessions' acquisition controls are `redsun`'s built-in ones: `acq_ctrl` is an `AcquisitionPresenter` (`redsun.presenter`) and `acq_widget` an `AcquisitionView` (`redsun.view.qt.builtins`). A session file pairs them with `pairs: [[acq_widget, acq_ctrl]]` and links `acq_ctrl.sig_plan_started` to `median_ctrl.clear_medians`.
